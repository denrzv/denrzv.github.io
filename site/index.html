Welcome, fellow optimist!

Tired of algorithms that work? Bored of O(n log n)? You have come to the right place. This homepage is dedicated to Bogosort, the sorting algorithm with the most faith in the universe:

  1. Look at the list.
  2. Is it sorted? Great, you're done!
  3. If not, throw it in the air, pick it all up again, and go to step 1.

Best case: O(n). Average case: O(n · n!). Worst case: the heat death of the universe, but at least you'll know exactly where you were.

Hall of Fame

Bogosort is not alone. These distinguished colleagues are honorary members:

AlgorithmHow it worksComplexity
Stalin SortWalk the list and quietly remove any element that is out of order. The list is now sorted. Nobody asks about the others.O(n), impressive
Sleep SortFor every number x, start a thread that sleeps x seconds, then prints x. Please do not sort phone numbers.O(max(x))
Miracle SortCheck whether the list is sorted. If not, wait a bit and check again. Cosmic rays will flip the right bits eventually.O(🙏)
Intelligent Design SortThe list is already in the order its creator intended. Who are you to question it?O(1)
Quantum BogosortShuffle the list once. If it is not sorted, destroy the universe. In the surviving universe, it is sorted.O(n), terms and conditions apply
BogobogosortBogosort the first two elements, then the first three, and so on, re-checking everything with more bogosorts. Strictly for the brave.Yes
